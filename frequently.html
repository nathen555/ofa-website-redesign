Frequently Asked Questions
Find answers to common questions about the Opportunities for All program.
Opportunities For All has placed more than 2,500 youth.
Inquiries should be sent to partners@opps4allsf.org.
Our nonprofit partner Japanese Community Youth Council (JCYC) is the employer of record and will process payroll, cover payroll taxes, insurance, etc.
JCYC works with students to set up direct deposit as part of their process for onboarding youth. Youth who need support setting up a bank account may contact ofa@jcyc.org.
​Businesses can sponsor the cost of an internship, host youth for a site visit, participate in a panel, provide a workshop for youth, or offer work-based learning opportunities, internships, or employment.
A list of sponsors may be found on our Sponsorship Page.
We can certainly work with other nonprofits but prefer that partners sponsor the initiative through JCYC.
If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us.
