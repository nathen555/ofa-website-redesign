▶ Am I required to have a resume?
A resume is a written account of your skills, experiences, and accomplishments. You will definitely need one! A resume helps you stand out when applying for jobs, internships, programs, college, or scholarships. It doesn't need to be fancy—everyday experiences like babysitting, selling homemade goods, or flipping sneakers can show responsibility and entrepreneurial skills. Use a template, add your info, and name the file with your first and last name.
▶ Why do I need a LinkedIn profile?
A LinkedIn profile boosts your visibility with employers and recruiters, builds your professional credibility, expands your network, and gives access to job opportunities.
