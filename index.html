ABOUT US
Opportunities for All (OFA) provides a pathway for young individuals to experience career exploration and workforce development in San Francisco.
This program includes paid internships, mentorship, and pathways to employment, including job readiness, career training, and apprenticeship opportunities.
OFA focuses on equitable access to these opportunities through workforce connection, support, and job resources for both jobseekers and employers.
